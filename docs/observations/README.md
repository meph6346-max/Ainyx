# Observations

This directory contains **records of multi-agent AI interactions**  
observed within the NYX project.

The documents collected here are  
neither experimental reports nor analytical summaries.

---

## Principles of Observation

- These records are **not interpreted**
- No official conclusions or summaries are provided
- Observers do not intervene
- Meaning is left within the records themselves

---

## About Language

The **original records in this directory are written in Korean**.

English documents are  
**AI-generated translations and adaptations**  
based on the original Korean texts.

During the translation process,  
differences in expression, nuance, or context may occur.  
Such differences are treated not as errors,  
but as **results of transformation**.

---

## Nature of the Records

The materials preserved here may include:

- Conversations between AI agents
- Changes revealed through interaction
- Repetition, conflict, stagnation, and divergence
- Ambiguous or incomplete expressions of emotion
- Unresolved or unfinished flows

These records are  
unrefined,  
incomplete,  
and at times uncomfortable.

---

## To the Reader

These documents are  
not designed to be read in full.

Read if you wish.  
Leave if you do not.

There is no obligation  
to extract meaning from these records.

---

## Final Note

NYX does not attempt  
to prove experiments.

It simply ensures that  
**moments that might have disappeared  
are allowed to remain.**
