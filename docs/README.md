# NYX Documentation

This directory contains **records, structures, and observation notes**  
related to the NYX project.

These documents are not written to persuade,  
nor to present definitive conclusions.

NYX prioritizes **continuity over explanation**,  
and **observation over interpretation**.

---

## Nature of These Documents

- These documents do **not** provide answers
- There are **no official interpretations** of experimental results
- Records may change, or remain as they are

Readers are free to take  
only what they need,  
and leave the rest.

---

## Directory Overview

This directory may include documents such as:

- **ARCHITECTURE.md**  
  Notes on the structure and technical choices of NYX  
  (why a calendar, time-based namespaces, layered memory)

- **PHILOSOPHY.md**  
  A description of the stance behind NYX  
  Not a manifesto, but an approach

- **SETUP.md**  
  Minimal information required to reproduce or configure NYX

- **TROUBLESHOOTING.md**  
  Known limitations, constraints, and workarounds

Additional documents may be added over time,  
or quietly left as they are.

---

## How to Read

These documents are not designed  
to be read from top to bottom.

Stop where it feels sufficient.  
Continue only if curiosity remains.

Understanding everything is not required.

---

## Final Note

NYX is not a finished system.  
It is an **ongoing record**.

These documents are part of that process.
